# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or reject contributions that do not meet them and may take appropriate corrective action.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/petersonPaloschi). Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Do not include API keys, private keys, or other sensitive data in a report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project in public.
